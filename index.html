Project Overview

Built as an internal-style procurement application concept, then converted here into a sanitized public portfolio demo. All values and names on this page are fictional.

The challenge

  • Procurement information is spread across operational spreadsheets.
  • Invoice and payment status need quick operational visibility.
  • Buyers need searchable purchasing history when evaluating repeat purchases.

The solution

  • A single control center with dashboard-style monitoring.
  • Invoice control and payment-status views with operational filters.
  • Purchase History search for PO, item, vendor, and site.

Key Features

The portfolio focuses on the practical functions that matter in procurement operations—not decorative dashboard elements.

01

Procurement Dashboard

Operational overview for PO workload, procurement status, site performance, and buyer activity.

02

Invoice Monitoring

Centralized invoice control, due-date visibility, payment status, outstanding values, and overdue monitoring.

03

Purchase History

Searchable purchasing history to compare previous purchases, suppliers, sites, quantities, and prices.

04

Buyer Monitoring

Buyer-level visibility designed to support workload tracking and procurement follow-up.

05

PO & Invoice Tracking

Connected operational views help users follow a PO from purchasing through invoice and payment.

06

Role-based Access

Designed with user roles in mind so operational screens can be presented according to responsibility.
